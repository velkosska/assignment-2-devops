Workshop desk

See what is free, what is out, and what is late. Then follow the three steps below.

Free to borrow … Out right now … Overdue …
  1. Add a tool

    Name it and give it a category. A retired tool, or one that is out, cannot be lent.

  2. Add a member

    A member needs a name and an email. The same email cannot be registered twice.

  3. Lend it for seven days

    Pick the member and a free tool. When it comes back, press Return. If it is still out after the due date, the row says Overdue. Nothing emails the member.
